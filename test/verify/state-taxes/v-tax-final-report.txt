Defects / unverifiable claims captured as skipped tests:
1. ACT land-tax 2026-27 rates apply before official 1 Jul 2026 start.
2. ACT land-tax rule effectiveFrom says 2026-01-01 while source says from 1 Jul 2026.
3. NT A$3m-under-A$5m duty applies 5.95%; official ready-reckoner evidence indicates 5.75%.
4. WA listed/public 10%-of-full-duty concession was not supported by official sources I could verify; source evidence only confirmed the 90% trigger.

Lead follow-up (19 Oct 2026): all four defects are fixed and their tests now run.
1-2. ACT: 2025-26 is modelled from the Taxation Administration (Amounts Payable—Land Tax) Determination 2025 (DI2025-153: fixed charge A$1,693, same rate table, 0.75% surcharge); the 2026-27 schedule and rule now start on 1 July 2026. For QLD, WA, SA, TAS and the ACT an assessment date from July to December counts toward the year ending the next 30 June.
3. NT: 5.75% from A$3m and below A$5m, 5.95% from A$5m, confirmed against the Stamp Duty Act 1978 (NT), Schedule 1 clause 1(2).
4. WA: the listed concession is removed. Duties Act 2008 (WA) s 184(1) charges the general rate and s 188(3) values the acquirer's interest; ss 190-192 were deleted with effect from 1 July 2008. Listed landholders now pay the general rate on the acquirer's interest.
