# Singapore (S-REIT): reference app vs this build

This page records how the Singapore (S-REIT) module compares with the reference app: what was proved against it, where this build deliberately differs, how rates and citations were checked, and what is still open.
The reference app is a separate managed-app prototype of the same product idea, used as a comparison target; this repository contains none of its source code.
No tax adviser has reviewed the rules in this repository.
The Australian equivalent is [au.md](au.md); [REFERENCE-PARITY.md](../REFERENCE-PARITY.md) lists the deliberate differences for both markets.

## Scope and evidence

- Scope: `src/domain/sg/**` (`sgModule`, all eleven `MarketModule` methods). Tests: `test/domain/sg/**`, 155 tests in eleven files plus `helpers.ts`, all passing at the time of writing (13 October 2026).
- Reference: the reference app's rendered pages for the four Singapore funds (Telok Ayer, Loyang, Meranti, Kallang) on all five fund workspaces, recorded on 12 October 2026 ("the reference pages"), and the reference app's front-end code (fund calculation, trial-balance derivation, notice checks, s45G rows and CSV, cost base).
- Rates and rules come from the engine (`src/engine/reitTax.ts`, the tested tax-maths layer described in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md)) and the cited law. Where they disagree with the reference app, the engine and the law win and the difference is listed in one of the two sections that follow "Matches the reference". Everything not listed matches the reference pages: labels, wording, ordering and numbers.
- The reference pages show whole dollars, so the tests compare a reference cell to within S$1; the s45G totals and the trial balance are asserted to the cent against the seed data. Money in this build is integer cents.
- The comparison tables use the columns Item, Reference app, This build and Evidence.

## Matches the reference

- Trial balance: the taxable specified income derived from the ledger equals `fund.inputs.taxableSpecifiedIncome` to the cent for all four funds, and every ledger is `balanced`. A distribution figure within 50 cents of the ledger's, 50 cents included, agrees, as in the reference. The book-to-tax result for Loyang is S$71,240,900 (`tb.test.ts`).
- Unmapped trial balance account: label "Needs a tax category", treatment "Excluded until mapped" (the reference's category table gives the same treatment for AU and SG). "Not part of taxable income" is the reference's treatment of the balance-sheet category, not of an unmapped account. `UNMAPPED_TREATMENT` in `tb.ts` is the one place to change it (`tb.test.ts`; `test/app/trial-balance/model.test.ts` uses the same text).
- Loyang: taxable specified income S$71,240,900, taxable distribution S$69,816,100, retained S$1,424,800, trustee tax S$242,216, tax deducted S$2,792,644 and reconciled against the amount remitted (`calc.test.ts`, `investor.test.ts`).
- Kallang fails the 90% test: payout 85.0%, retained S$6,286,000, trustee tax S$1,068,620, one exception. The exception's detail is the reference's text plus one sentence giving the engine's worst case, S$7,124,105, and the S$837,082 of withholding that would then fall away (see "Where the reference and the law disagree and the reference's figures are shown"). Telok Ayer and Meranti carry two notes each; Loyang has none (`calc.test.ts`).
- Holder rate and destination labels are the reference's: "Exempt · paid gross", "Paid gross · taxed at own rate", "10% final", "17% · not final"; destinations "Paid gross", "Non-residents & others, net", "Withheld for IRAS", "Units not on register" (`calc.test.ts`).
- s45G table, tax deducted: Telok Ayer S$1,869,203.20, Loyang S$2,792,644.00, Meranti S$1,529,560.80, Kallang S$837,081.75. Rates read "10% final" and "17% not final". Loyang's notice is published on 30 Jul 2026. Due dates: Loyang 15 Oct 2026; the other three 15 Jan 2027 (`investor.test.ts`).
- Notice checks use the reference's ids and titles: register, payout, over-distribution, withholding reconciliation, no-declaration, notice late, notice due, notice changed (50 cents or more) and withholding overdue. The details are the reference's except three: the payout detail has one extra sentence (see "Where the reference and the law disagree and the reference's figures are shown"), and the notice-late and notice-due details say that the declaration cut-off and the notice date are the manager's, not IRAS's (see "Where the law or the engine wins") (`calc.test.ts`, `investor.test.ts`).
- Deadlines at 12 Oct 2026 09:00: the open SG rows (three notices, three withholding dates, four Form UT dates) are the ones in the reference figures by date, fund and obligation, and none is overdue. Loyang's notice and withholding are done (`deadlines.test.ts`). Each fund has the same three obligations in the reference's order: distribution tax notice, withholding tax (s45G) and Form UT. There is no row for the Form A and Form B declarations: the declaration cut-off is the manager's date, not an IRAS date, and appears on the notice. The row text is the reference's even though the notice row sits at the manager's record date, which is not an IRAS date (see "Where the law or the engine wins").
- Tax leakage and distribution mix equal the reference figures for all four funds, with percentages to one decimal place; capital gains are not a separate Singapore bucket (`analytics.test.ts`).
- Distribution scenario, Loyang at 85%: taxable distribution S$60,554,765, retained S$10,686,135, trustee tax S$1,816,643, withholding S$2,422,191, 90% condition "No" (the reference's figures, checked on the running reference app at this one input; the recorded pages show only the Australian scenario). At 90% it passes: S$64,116,810, S$7,124,090, S$1,211,095, S$2,564,672, "Yes" (`analytics.test.ts`).
- Cost base: the property and unit rows, the totals and the two CSV layouts follow the reference. Kallang's property cost S$4,764,348,382, valuation S$4,892,269,910 and unrealised gain S$127,921,528 were compared with the reference pages when the module was written (a one-off check; the suite asserts only that totals agree with rows and with the capital distributed, and Loyang's first CSV row) (`costbase.test.ts`).
- Cost base polish: the property panel now shows the reference note when valuations and the trial balance's investment-properties balance differ by S$0.50 or more, worded "Valuations total X; investment properties in the trial balance are Y." The units table carries an "Excess over cost base" field on each SG row; seeded holders have no excess, so seed output is unchanged (`costbase.test.ts`, `test/app/cost-base/model.test.ts`).
- Tracking schedule: rows are the income types and the columns are Opening, Derived, Distributed, Transferred and Closing, as on the reference pages. The IRAS Annex 6 template is the transposed layout (e-Tax Guide Annex 6; `returns.test.ts`).
- Statements: each of the 20 SG holders has a unitholder statement, and each agrees with the fund calculation to the cent (`statements.test.ts`).

Notes:

1. Declaration cut-off wording. In the reference, the notice's date label reads "Form A and Form B due" and its paragraph "Unitholders other than individuals submit Form A, and depository agents Form B, by 13 Nov 2026 to be paid gross or at 10%. …"; the notice-due detail reads "Form A and Form B declarations are due 16 Nov 2026." In this build the label is "Declaration cut-off set by the manager"; the paragraph is "IRAS requires unitholders other than individuals to submit Form A, and depository agents Form B, before every distribution (IRAS e-Tax Guide para 13.1). The declaration cut-off set by the manager is 13 Nov 2026: submit by then to be paid gross or at 10%. Without a declaration, tax is deducted at 17%."; and the notice-due detail is "Declaration cut-off set by the manager: 16 Nov 2026. IRAS requires Form A (depository agents: Form B) before every distribution (e-Tax Guide para 13.1); announcing the notice before the record date is market practice, not an IRAS rule." The seed data is fictional and unchanged. Evidence: e-Tax Guide ¶13.1(a) has unit holders (other than individuals) submit Form A to the trustee "prior to every distribution". The Guide sets no cut-off of seven days after the record date: a text search of the 12th edition finds no "7 days" or "seven days", and "record date" only in a footnote on approved sub-trusts. ¶13.1(b) names Form B but does not restate its timing, and ¶12.2(b) lets ultimate beneficiaries confirm once; applying "before every distribution" to both forms is this build's reading of the Guide.
2. Tax notice timing. The reference's notice-late detail reads "Record date 5 Nov 2026. Unitholders need the tax breakdown to submit their declarations." In this build the notice-late detail adds the Form A and Form B sentence: "Record date 5 Nov 2026. Unitholders need the tax breakdown to submit their declarations. IRAS requires Form A (depository agents: Form B) before every distribution (e-Tax Guide para 13.1); announcing the notice before the record date is market practice, not an IRAS rule." A Rules row, "Declaration cut-off and tax notice timing" (see "Not in the reference (our design)"), says the same, and a comment in `deadlines.ts` records why the row is dated at the record date.
3. Return of capital. This build's footnote reads "Unit holders should reduce their cost of units by the amount of return of capital (ITA s35(15C)). For traders, the reduced cost is used for trading gains; if the return of capital exceeds the cost, the excess is taxable as trading income and the proceeds of all later sales of the remaining units are fully taxable." The Rules row has the same text without the inline citation. The sentence this module adds for a holder over cost ("Cost can't fall below nil, so the closing cost base is nil. Excess over cost base: …") still follows the footnote.

## Where the reference and the law disagree and the reference's figures are shown

When the 90% distribution condition fails, the trustee-tax figure this build displays is the reference app's simplification: 17% of the retained income only (Kallang S$1,068,620; Loyang at 85% S$1,816,643). The law (IRAS e-Tax Guide ¶18.5) taxes all of the taxable specified income at the trustee when the 90% condition is not met; for Kallang that worst case is S$7,124,105. This build keeps the reference's figures as the displayed primary numbers and states the law beside them: the payout exception carries the worst-case figure, and the Rules tab states the rule and points to that figure. The citations are listed under the table.

Note 1. The sentence added to the payout exception: "If the Comptroller denies tax transparency for the year, the trustee would instead be taxed on all of the taxable specified income at 17%: S$7,124,105 (the engine's worst case), and no s45G withholding would apply to distributions out of income already taxed at the trustee (IRAS e-Tax Guide para 8.4), so the S$837,082 of withholding computed here would fall away; this needs a tax adviser's view." The rate and the trustee-tax amount come from `computeReitTax` (`trusteeTaxRateBps`, `trusteeTax`) and the withholding is the fund's computed withholding (`calc.withheld`), all through the shared money helpers; the amount follows the rate so that it reads as the tax, not as the income. When nothing is withheld, the "so the S$… of withholding computed here would fall away" clause is left out.

Citations for the failing-90% treatment (IRAS e-Tax Guide on REITs unless stated):

- ITA s43(2) (¶5.1).
- ¶5.3(a): transparency is "subject to" distributing at least 90% of taxable income to unit holders in the same year.
- ¶6.1: where transparency applies, the trustee is taxed on the specified income not distributed in the same year and on other income.
- ¶18.5: for a REIT that cannot meet the 90% requirement "tax transparency treatment will not apply, and the full amount of the taxable specified income will be taxed in the hands of the trustee" (worded for the COVID-19 extended period, but the clearest statement of the consequence).
- ¶10.4: the approved treatment ceases "from the date any of the conditions imposed is not met".
- ¶8.4: "Distributions made out of income that has been taxed on the trustee will not be subject to further tax when distributed to the unit holders".

Engine check: no engine fault was found, so there is no failing engine test. The differences above are not engine faults: the engine's conservative reading agrees with e-Tax Guide ¶18.5 and ¶8.4, and the reference's retained-only figures are the simplification. The engine's reading is stated beside the reference's figures on the Checks and Rules tabs.

## Rounding

- Money is integer cents; percentages are rounded only when shown (payout and mix to one decimal place).
- Units are shared by `units / max(units on issue, register units)`, as in the reference, with the remainder cents given by largest remainder. The part belonging to units that are not on the register is its own bucket, so the holders and the bucket add up to each component to the cent. The engine's `allocateToUnitholders` shares over the register only, so the fund calculation does not use it. The engine's `allocate` agreed with this build's allocation on the seed and on over 320,000 random cases, checked outside the suite, so this is not an engine fault.
- Taxable specified income from the ledger is rounded to a whole dollar and never negative, as in the reference. Reconciling lines under half a dollar are left out. The ledger is balanced when the total is under half a dollar. A distribution within half a dollar of the ledger's figure, half a dollar included, agrees with it, as in the reference.
