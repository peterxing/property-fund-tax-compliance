# Australia (AMIT): reference app vs this build

This page records how the Australian (AMIT and MIT) module compares with the reference app: what was proved against it, where this build deliberately differs, how rates and citations were checked, and what is still open.
The reference app is a separate managed-app prototype of the same product idea, used as a comparison target; this repository contains none of its source code.
No tax adviser has reviewed the rules in this repository.
The Singapore equivalent is [sg.md](sg.md); [REFERENCE-PARITY.md](../REFERENCE-PARITY.md) lists the deliberate differences for both markets.

## Scope and evidence

- Scope: `src/domain/au/**` (`auModule`, all eleven `MarketModule` methods). Tests: `test/domain/au/**`, 12 files plus `helpers.ts` and `papersCapture.ts`; 174 tests, all passing at the time of writing (13 October 2026).
- Reference: the reference app's rendered pages for the five Australian funds (Harbourline, Southbank, Wattle, Kurrajong, Coastline) on all five fund workspaces, recorded on 12 October 2026 ("the reference pages"), and the reference app's front-end code.
- Rates and rules come from the engine (`src/engine/au/**`, the tested tax-maths layer described in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md)) and the cited law. Where they disagree with the reference app, the engine and the law win and the difference is listed under "Where the law or the engine wins". Everything not listed matches the reference pages: labels, wording, ordering and numbers.
- The reference pages show whole dollars, so the tests compare each cell to within A$1. Money in this build is integer cents.
- The comparison tables use the columns Item, Reference app, This build and Evidence.

## Matches the reference

- Trial balance: `derivedInputs` equals `fund.inputs` to the cent for all five funds, and every trial balance is `balanced`. Harbourline's "Net income attributed to members" is A$59,620,600 (`tb.test.ts`).
- Book-to-tax title: "Reconciliation of accounting profit to taxable income" on all five funds (`tb.test.ts`).
- Unmapped trial balance account: label "Needs a tax category", treatment "Excluded until mapped" (Coastline account 4510 on the reference pages; the reference's category table gives the same treatment for AU and SG). "Not part of taxable income" is the reference's treatment of the balance-sheet category, not of an unmapped account. `UNMAPPED_TREATMENT` in `tb.ts` is the one place to change it (`tb.test.ts`; `test/app/trial-balance/model.test.ts` expects the same text).
- Distribution: gross, net and withheld for every fund. Harbourline's per-holder net and withheld equal the reference figures (`calc.test.ts`).
- Southbank's four checks equal the reference figures: AMMA overdue; withholding computed A$3,892,099 against A$2,410,099 remitted (under by A$1,482,000); cost base shortfall A$4,816,860 (0.7040 c/unit); notices understate the fund payment by A$19,760,000 (`calc.test.ts`, `investor.test.ts`). The cost base check's detail names the AMIT CGT gross-up amount where the reference says "the CGT concession" (see the next section).
- Wattle: zero exceptions and zero variance, with A$2,742,646 withheld from its foreign holders and all of it remitted (`calc.test.ts`).
- Deferred tax for the EOI investor: Harbourline 28,926,024; Southbank 41,589,080; Kurrajong 40,151,760; Coastline 10,362,870; Wattle 0 (`costbase.test.ts`).
- Deadlines at 12 Oct 2026 09:00: the AU rows equal the reference figures in date, fund, obligation and status (`deadlines.test.ts`). Each fund has the same four obligations: AMMA statements, AIIR, fund payment notice (while unpublished) and fund payment withholding report. There are no tax-return or remittance rows.
- AIIR due dates: AIIR due 31 Oct 2026; "Fund payment withholding report" due 14 Jan 2027. The AIIR date is 31 October after a 30 June year end, a constant in `investor.ts` because the engine states it only as rule text. The withholding report is due on `mitAiirDueDate` (6 months and 14 days after the year end: 14 Jan, or 14 Apr for Coastline). Sources: ATO AIIR due-date page; TAA 1953 Sch 1 s393-10 (the period the Commissioner specifies, otherwise 4 months; an MIT must lodge whatever its investor count, s393-10(5A)); TAA Sch 1 s16-153(4) and (4A) (withholding report) (`deadlines.test.ts`, `investor.test.ts`).
- AIIR due date for a substituted accounting period (Coastline): the reference shows "Confirm with the ATO — substituted accounting period" instead of a date, and the withholding report is due 14 Apr 2027. Here `aiir.aiirDue` is `""` (an `ISODate` cannot hold text), `aiir.note` carries the text and there is no AIIR deadline row; the Investor reporting workspace shows the note when `aiirDue` is empty. The ATO gives 31 October for 30 June year ends only (`investor.test.ts`, `deadlines.test.ts`).
- Notices against the year (Coastline): four columns (published, draft notices, the year's components, difference). "Fund payment — other" reads A$12,645,598 published, A$4,324,852 draft, A$16,970,450 year and a difference of A$0. The domain's `vsYear` rows carry no draft column, so their `difference` is `year − published` (A$4,324,852 for "Fund payment — other" and A$54,104 for "Interest"); the Investor reporting workspace adds the draft column and nets it off. Coastline's draft has no snapshot (`notified` is null), so its amounts are estimated as the balance of the year not yet notified (`auNoticeAmounts`), which equals the reference's draft column and the notice document's table (`investor.test.ts`).
- "Trust components not reported against an investor" (Kurrajong A$53,051): the reference shows a row under "Total reported" in the AIIR lines table. Here the amount is in `aiir.items[2].detail` ("A$53,051 of attributed amounts isn't reported against an investor") and equals the sum of `unallocated` over the attributed components of `FundCalc.components`; the Investor reporting workspace adds the row when it is above zero (`investor.test.ts`).
- Draft notice: the reference shows a DRAFT badge beside the unpublished year-end notice; the domain sets `isCurrentYearDraft` on the list entry and titles the document "Fund payment notice (draft)" (`investor.test.ts`).
- Tax returns working papers: all five funds read line for line like the reference pages, with the same headings, row order and whole-dollar figures. Harbourline's "Assessable income" is A$55,708,600 and includes "Clean building MIT income" A$6,205,300. That row and the foreign income row show only when the fund has some (the reference pages show them for Harbourline and Coastline only). The recorded page text is in `test/domain/au/papersCapture.ts` (`returns.test.ts`).
- Tax leakage and mix equal the reference figures for all five funds (`analytics.test.ts`).
- Distribution scenario: Harbourline reads A$92,446,000, A$28,913,400 excess, 2.0467 and A$59,620,600 in both columns. Every AU fund's current column carries the cost base net amount and c/unit that the Distribution tab shows: 2.0467, 0.7040, 0.6854, 1.8560 and 0.8655 for the five funds in order. Southbank's A$4,816,860 shortfall reads −0.7040 in the scenario (signed) and 0.7040 under a "Shortfall" label on the Distribution tab. The labels and the note are the reference's (`analytics.test.ts`).
- Fund-level and register-level figures: Kurrajong's register holds 950,150,000 of 951,400,000 units. `FundCalc` and the scenario are fund-level (the Distribution tab shows an AMIT cost base net amount excess of A$17,658,200). The AIIR and the unit cost base are register-level (excess A$17,635,000, and A$53,051 of attributed amounts not reported against an investor). The reference shows the same two sets of figures on the same tabs, so this is not a difference.

Notes:

1. The new name follows through everywhere the module writes it: the components table and each investor's lines, the AIIR CSV header, the statement's component row and its footnote ("…after taking out the AMIT CGT gross-up amount of A$586,800.00…"), the Rules tab's cost base row, and the Southbank cost base check ("Attributed amounts and the AMIT CGT gross-up amount exceed the cash distribution by A$4,816,860 (0.7040 c/unit); members increase their cost base."). The Sankey short label is "CGT gross-up". Source: the ATO's AMMA statement and SDS 2024 guidance notes (a page the engine cites) give "AMIT CGT gross up amount (AMMA statement) and CGT concession amount (SDS)". For the AMMA statement the AMIT CGT gross up amount is the additional amount "treated as capital gains of members under subsections 276-85(3) and (4) of the ITAA 1997, and included in the AMIT cost base increase amount under subsection 104-107E(4)". "CGT concession amount" is the non-AMIT (SDS) term. `calc.test.ts` covers ten labels and shorts for all five funds and the Southbank check.
2. The same ATO guidance notes: "Other non-attributable amounts (AMMA statement) are cash distributions and other entitlements from an AMIT … that exceed the attribution amount". Tax-deferred and tax-free amounts are the non-AMIT (SDS) categories they broadly correspond to, and the AMIT cost base net amount takes their place.
3. LCR 2020/2 para 251 (ATO legal database): for an existing facility already in use and producing income, the transitional rules apply to amounts derived, received or made before 1 July 2026, or before 1 July 2034 for an economic infrastructure facility. The engine's rule notes ([au-trusts-mit.md](../rules/au-trusts-mit.md)) and `src/engine/au` carry neither date, so the ruling is cited directly.

## Found by tests and review (fixed)

- Engine, `ammaDueDate` in `src/engine/au/amit.ts`: the reference pages show Coastline's AMMA statements due 31 Dec 2026 (30 September year end). The engine used to return 30 Dec 2026 because it added three calendar months to the year end. It now reckons the period from the day after the year end, so a year ending on the last day of a month finishes on the last day of the month three on. The same gap had affected year ends of 30 April (30 Jul instead of 31 Jul) and 28 or 29 February (28 or 29 May instead of 31 May). `ammaDue` in `calc.ts` uses the engine's date. Sources: ITAA 1997 s276-455(1) ("within 3 months after the end of the income year"); Acts Interpretation Act 1901 s36(1) item 6 (a period that begins after a day excludes that day) and s2G(2) (a period of months starting 1 October ends immediately before 1 January). Tests: `test/engine/au/amit.test.ts`; `util.test.ts` (30 September, February and April year ends, and `ammaDue` agreeing with the engine).
- `costBaseView().cgtSummary` could return `-0` for a nil row. The purity test in `invariants.test.ts`, which sweeps every view, found it, and `nz()` now normalises it.
- A nil AMIT cost base net amount reads "AMIT cost base net amount — nil" on the statement rather than "excess" or "shortfall".
- The scenario's "Per unit (cents)" row printed cash per unit (Harbourline 6.5440). Comparison with the reference showed that its row is the AMIT cost base net amount per unit (Harbourline 2.0467 in both columns). `scenario()` now divides the cost base net amount by the units on issue, and `analytics.test.ts` pins it. The 6.5440 figure is correct where it belongs, in the Distribution tab's "Total by tax character" row.
- A code comment in `rates.ts` cited s5 of the Income Tax (Dividends, Interest and Royalties Withholding Tax) Act 1974 for the interest rate. It is s7(b).
- `workingPapers` in `returns.ts` left the `cleanBuilding` component out of its rows, subtotal and total. Harbourline's "Assessable income" read A$49,503,300 instead of A$55,708,600 and the "Clean building MIT income" row (A$6,205,300) was missing, on screen and in the PDF, which is built from the same document. The row now follows "Australian income — other", as on the reference pages. No other fund's papers changed (compared before and after). The first Harbourline pin in `returns.test.ts` had recorded the wrong total; it now pins the reference, and every fund's papers are compared line by line with the recorded reference text. `completeness.test.ts` adds property tests over 185 scenarios (each fund as seeded, with each of the nine inputs raised, cleared and set alone, with every input cleared, and with eight random sets): every attributed component with an amount appears exactly once in the working papers, the notice tables and the member statements, and their totals, the fund payment and the withholding tie to `FundCalc`. Putting the omission back makes them fail. The notice tables and member statements already listed every component, so only the papers needed a fix.

## Known gaps

- Trial balance, "Distribution components" table: the reference lists the cash distribution as a row. This build lists only the components the trial balance can derive (`TbView.components`). The cash distribution is a decision, not a trial-balance output, so the "Update distribution" sync never overwrites it, and it is not shown as a row there (`tb.test.ts`).
- The deferred tax figures for a non-EOI foreign investor, a complying super fund and a company have no reference value; they come from the engine and the law (see "Deferred tax by investor type").
- Whether a payment is an eligible build-to-rent fund payment is not decided here: the build-to-rent Rules row is informational and no investor line uses the rate.
- The AMIT member annual statement is this build's own layout, because the reference pages carry no content for it.
